# Sangwon Lee

Robotics and vision software engineer. I work on humanoid-robot vision and policy learning, after several years building production robot-vision and AI inspection systems for manufacturing lines.

[CV](https://sangwon2659.github.io/cv/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/s-won-lee/) · [Email](mailto:sangwon2659@gmail.com)

## What I work on

**Now — vision and policy learning for humanoid manipulation.** Multi-modal VLA (Vision-Language-Action) architectures and staged training on multi-node GPU clusters; offline advantage-conditioned policy post-training and reinforcement learning for manipulation policies; real-time policy execution and speed acceleration; detection with custom keypoint heads; robust classical computer vision for cases where a learned model is not the right answer.

**Before — production robot vision and AI inspection.** Bin-picking perception, palletizing vision, 2D position correction for industrial robots, stereo vision, and deep-learning visual inspection systems deployed to live manufacturing lines.

**Background.** M.S. in Mechanical Engineering, Yonsei University (Precision Control Systems Laboratory). Thesis on slip detection for robotic grippers from normal-force sensor arrays, published in *Sensors and Materials*.

## Technical focus

**AI / deep learning** — PyTorch · Hugging Face Transformers · LeRobot · VLA fusion architectures · advantage-conditioned policy learning · YOLO · Mask2Former · DETR · SigLIP · multi-node distributed training

**Robotics & real-time execution** — ROS · ROS2 · action chunking and asynchronous inference · optimization-based action post-processing · KUKA industrial robots · Robotis Dynamixel

**Vision** — structured-light, ToF, and stereo cameras · camera-to-robot calibration · homography estimation · RANSAC variants · hierarchical template matching · OpenCV

**Reading** — I keep notes on VLA and RL-for-robotics papers: π₀ family, OpenVLA, RT-2 / RT-X, GR00T N1, Helix, and recent work on real-time execution and demonstration speedup.
